TOKYO – June 10, 2026 – Geekplus Co., Ltd. has announced the deployment of moving-type Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs) at several Toyota Motor Corporation plants in Japan. Currently, a total of 436 Geekplus moving-type AMRs are operating in Toyota Motor Corporation's factories, with operations scaled up to approximately 200 units per system.
